Chongrui Ye

I study how language agents learn
and make use of experience.

I’m an undergraduate in Statistics & Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign. Previously, I studied Applied Mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen before transferring to UIUC. My research interests include reinforcement learning, agent memory, and the evaluation of language agents.

I work with Prof. Andrea Zanette at Carnegie Mellon University on off-policy RL. Previously, I worked with Prof. Jiaxuan You at UIUC’s uLab and Prof. Baoxiang Wang at CUHK–Shenzhen.
